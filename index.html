FlightLander — Privacy Policy

Effective 12 November 2026

FlightLander is a virtual reality game for Meta Quest headsets. This policy explains what information the game collects, why, who processes it, and how you can control or delete it.

The short version: FlightLander does not collect your name, email address, Meta account, voice, photos or location. It does not show ads and does not sell or share data for advertising. Gameplay analytics are sent only if you say yes, and you can switch them off or delete them from inside the game at any time. Online multiplayer, when you use it, needs an anonymous player ID and passes your connection through Unity's relay servers.

Who we are

FlightLander is published by Gabriel Machado de Carvalho Gomes (HyperbolicMinds), the data controller for the purposes of this policy. Contact: gabriel.machado.cg@gmail.com.

1. Information that stays on your headset

Your settings and progress (comfort options, menu choices, your analytics answer) are stored on the headset only. Some builds also write a performance log to the headset's own storage. None of this is uploaded; it is removed when you uninstall the game.

2. Gameplay analytics (only with your consent)

The first time you play, the game asks whether you want to share anonymous gameplay data. If you decline, nothing in this section is collected. If you agree, the game sends the following to Unity Gaming Services Analytics so we can find levels that are too hard, routes that are confusing, and performance problems:

WhatExamples
Level and tutorial progresslevel name, win or loss and its reason, time played, tutorial step reached or skipped
Flightsship type, how a flight ended, flight time and distance, the shape of the route you drew and the path flown, number of route edits, crash cause
Settingsthe comfort preset you chose
Performanceaverage and worst frame rate, number of hitches
Added by the Unity SDKa random installation/player ID, device model, operating system and app version, language, and approximate country derived from your IP address

The ID is random and generated by the game; it is not linked to your Meta account or your name. The legal basis is your consent.

Your controls: in Settings > PRIVACY you can stop sharing at any time, and DELETE MY DATA asks Unity to erase all analytics data held for your device (it also stops further sharing).

3. Online multiplayer (only when you host or join)

Solo play makes no multiplayer connections. When you host or join an online session, the game uses Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Multiplayer Sessions and Relay):

  • Anonymous player ID, created by Unity Authentication on your first online session and kept on the headset. No account, name or email is involved.
  • Session data: the session code and the IDs of the players in it, held by Unity while the session exists. Sessions are private and never listed publicly.
  • IP address: all game traffic passes through Unity's relay servers, which therefore see each player's IP address. Other players never see your IP address.
  • Game traffic: head and controller positions, drawn routes and ship state, used only to run the shared game. There is no voice chat, text chat or user-chosen name.

This is needed to provide the multiplayer feature you asked for (legal basis: performance of the service you requested). It is not used for analytics or advertising.

4. Who processes the data

Analytics and multiplayer are provided by Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services), which acts as our processor and may process data outside your country under appropriate safeguards. See Unity's privacy policy. We do not sell personal data or share it with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

Meta collects its own data about your use of the Quest platform and store under Meta's privacy policy; FlightLander does not receive that data.

5. Retention

Analytics data is kept only as long as it is useful for improving the game, and is deleted when you use DELETE MY DATA or ask us to. Multiplayer session data exists only while the session does; Unity's own logs follow Unity's retention policy.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, Brazil's LGPD or California's CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your data, and to withdraw consent. The quickest way is the in-game controls above, which act on your device's data directly; you can also email us. Because we do not know who you are, we may need details from you to find your data. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Children

FlightLander is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has shared data with us, email us and we will delete it.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be shown in the game.
